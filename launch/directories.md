## Sidewise

One-liner: What is the other side reading?

Description: Sidewise pulls feeds from 14 newsrooms across the spectrum, clusters same-day stories, tags each left, center or right, and flags blindspots stories only one side is covering. Reader plus a free API and MCP server. Free on web and Mac.

Category: News / Developer Tools
